Captions (D9) — alt text → caption, off-Obsidian

A CLAIMED image with non-empty alt gets a plain-text caption below it, sized to the image width. Markdown in the alt is NOT rendered off-Obsidian (no platform renderer) — it shows literally (a documented degradation). A NOT-claimed (width-only) image gets no caption even with alt — add .lie to claim it.
